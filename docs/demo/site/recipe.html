Weeknight Pasta e Ceci

A Roman pantry supper: chickpeas, a little pasta, and enough broth to make it a soup. Thirty minutes, one pot.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 sprig rosemary
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 can (400 g) chickpeas, drained
  • 1 litre vegetable broth
  • 150 g ditalini
  • Pecorino and black pepper to finish

Method

  1. Warm the oil with the garlic and rosemary until the garlic is golden.
  2. Stir in the tomato paste for a minute, then the chickpeas and broth.
  3. Simmer ten minutes, crush a third of the chickpeas, add the pasta.
  4. Cook until the pasta is tender and the soup is thick. Finish with pecorino.
